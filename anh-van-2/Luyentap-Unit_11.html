Lưu ý trước khi làm bài

Vui lòng tuân thủ các quy định sau trong suốt quá trình làm bài:

  • Không sử dụng cùng một tài khoản trên nhiều thiết bị hoặc trình duyệt.
  • Không chuyển thẻ (tab) hoặc mở cửa sổ khác khi đang làm bài.
  • Không tải lại trang hoặc đóng trình duyệt trước khi hoàn thành bài kiểm tra.

Nếu vi phạm bất kỳ quy định nào ở trên, hệ thống có thể không ghi nhận kết quả bài thi.

Our new office

We used to have a lot of walls in our office. We divided the space into different areas for different teams, and managers had their own private offices to work in. But when the new CEO came in, she decided to make a change. We worked at home for a week, and when we came back, we saw that the walls had been taken down, and we now had one big room. The CEO explained that this would be better for working in teams. We would be able to talk to each other more easily and feel like part of the same big team, rather than just part of lots of small teams.

It's a nice idea, but not everyone agrees with this approach. In fact, some people think that it's much harder to get work done in a big open-plan space like this. One problem is that there are more things going on around you. You can see more people walking past, and you can hear more conversations. Another problem is that some staff members do not feel comfortable with having their manager sitting nearby. They feel that the manager can see everything they do all day, and this makes them nervous.

I can see both sides. I like being able to ask my teammates a quick question without sending an email, but sometimes it is hard to concentrate when people are talking nearby. Maybe the new office is better for teamwork, and the old office was better for quiet work. At the moment, I'm really not sure which style is better.
