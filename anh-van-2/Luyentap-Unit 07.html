Lưu ý trước khi làm bài

Vui lòng tuân thủ các quy định sau trong suốt quá trình làm bài:

  • Không sử dụng cùng một tài khoản trên nhiều thiết bị hoặc trình duyệt.
  • Không chuyển thẻ (tab) hoặc mở cửa sổ khác khi đang làm bài.
  • Không tải lại trang hoặc đóng trình duyệt trước khi hoàn thành bài kiểm tra.

Nếu vi phạm bất kỳ quy định nào ở trên, hệ thống có thể không ghi nhận kết quả bài thi.

Do you hate housework?

Did you know that the average person spends more than 600 hours a year (or 12.5 hours a week) doing housework? So perhaps it's no surprise that most people hate it.

The jobs that people like the least are washing the dishes, doing the laundry, cleaning the bathroom, vacuuming, and going food shopping. And when people share a house together, they often argue about who does different types of housework. In fact, 80% of the couples we asked say they have argued about housework. 61% admit they have recleaned something because they think their partner did a bad job, and 67% admit that they have done a bad job so they are not asked to do it again.

Because housework often causes arguments, 33% of the people surveyed pay a house cleaner to come to their home. And 62% believe that doing this has helped their relationship with their partner. However, hiring a house cleaner costs money, so people have to decide what's more important: their time or their money.
