Lưu ý trước khi làm bài

Vui lòng tuân thủ các quy định sau trong suốt quá trình làm bài:

  • Không sử dụng cùng một tài khoản trên nhiều thiết bị hoặc trình duyệt.
  • Không chuyển thẻ (tab) hoặc mở cửa sổ khác khi đang làm bài.
  • Không tải lại trang hoặc đóng trình duyệt trước khi hoàn thành bài kiểm tra.

Nếu vi phạm bất kỳ quy định nào ở trên, hệ thống có thể không ghi nhận kết quả bài thi.

Pet cafés

Do you want a pet, but you can't have one because you don't have enough space at home? Then perhaps you should visit a pet café. In many cities around the world, pet cafés are becoming very popular. However, not everyone agrees they're a good thing.

A pet café is not a normal café or coffee shop. Although most (but not all) sell food and drinks, the main attraction is the animals. Customers pay a fee to visit the pet café for a set amount of time (usually an hour). During that time, they can spend time in a room full of animals. They can watch them, play with them, and touch them. The most popular kind is a cat café.

Cats are not the only animals you can find. Some pet cafés have unusual animals, such as owls, snakes, and even hedgehogs, as well as more typical pets like dogs and rabbits.

Not everyone is happy about this trend. Some people think that the animals need a larger place to live, and that they can become stressed when so many different people touch them every day. Others worry about the health of the animals and the customers. Because the rules are different in every country, I think that all countries should have clear rules for pet cafés. That way, the animals will be safe, and customers can enjoy their visit.
